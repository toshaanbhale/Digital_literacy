# 🛡️ Stay Safe Online  
### 📌 Prevention Checklist for College Students (India)

---

## ✅ Do’s (Follow These)

| ✔️ Action | 📖 Description |
|----------|--------------|
| Use strong passwords | Create passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols |
| Enable 2FA | Add extra security to important accounts |
| Verify links | Check sender before clicking links or emails |
| Update devices | Keep software and apps up to date |
| Check privacy settings | Limit who can see your personal information |
| Logout on shared devices | Prevent unauthorized access |

---

## ❌ Don’ts (Avoid These)

| ❌ Risk | ⚠️ Why to Avoid |
|--------|----------------|
| Sharing OTP/passwords | Leads to account hacking |
| Clicking unknown links | May install malware or phishing |
| Accepting unknown requests | Can expose personal data |
| Downloading from unsafe sites | Risk of viruses and scams |

---

## 💳 UPI / Financial Safety

| 💰 Tip | 📌 Key Point |
|-------|-------------|
| Do not approve unknown requests | Fraudsters send fake payment requests |
| No PIN needed to receive money | Enter PIN ONLY to send money |
| Avoid random QR codes | Can trigger unauthorized payments |
| Verify receiver details | Always confirm before paying |

---

## 🔒 Final Tip

> **Think before you click. Verify before you trust. Stay safe online.**
