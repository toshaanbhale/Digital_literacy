# 📱 Social Media Do's and Don'ts Checklist

## 👤 For College Students – Responsible Digital Behavior

---

## ✅ Do's

- ✔️ **Think before you post** – Ensure your content is appropriate and respectful.
- ✔️ **Maintain privacy settings** – Regularly check and update your account security.
- ✔️ **Be respectful and professional** – Communicate politely in comments and messages.
- ✔️ **Verify information before sharing** – Avoid spreading misinformation or fake news.
- ✔️ **Build a positive digital footprint** – Share achievements, skills, and constructive content.

---

## ❌ Don'ts

- ❌ **Do not share personal or sensitive information** – Avoid posting phone numbers, addresses, etc.
- ❌ **Avoid cyberbullying or offensive language** – Respect others' opinions and feelings.
- ❌ **Do not post inappropriate content** – This can harm your reputation and future opportunities.
- ❌ **Avoid oversharing** – Keep a balance between online and offline life.
- ❌ **Do not click on suspicious links** – Protect yourself from scams and phishing attacks.

---

## ⚠️ Real-Life Scenario (Digital Communication Issue)

A college student once posted a sarcastic comment about a group project member on social media without mentioning their name directly. However, classmates quickly identified the person being referred to. This led to misunderstandings, hurt feelings, and conflicts within the group, ultimately affecting their project performance.

This situation could have been handled better by addressing the issue privately and professionally rather than posting it publicly. Effective communication and mutual respect are essential to maintaining healthy relationships both online and offline.

---

## 📝 Conclusion

Using social media responsibly is essential for maintaining a positive image and ensuring safe digital interactions. By following these do's and don'ts, students can create a healthy and respectful online presence.
